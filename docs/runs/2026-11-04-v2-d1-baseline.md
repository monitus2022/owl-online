  question names the book and chapter. That is the D3 target, and multi-hop does
  not explain it.
- EM is 0.00 across the board. Answers are sentences and references are short
  noun phrases, so EM measures brevity here. Set D2 thresholds on F1, judge
  buckets and faithfulness, not on EM.
- Faithfulness is 11/15, and all four failures sit outside `simple` (2 in
  named_reference, 2 in multi_part). Unsupported claims appear when an answer
  stitches two facts together.
- Abstention is at ceiling (10/10) with no abstention in the judged sample, so
  there is no measured over-refusal to buy back in D6.
- Not comparable with the V1 mini-eval (Hit@5 0.70). The V2 gold set is larger
  and includes the named and multi-part slices V1 did not have.
