## Reading

- `named_reference` is the weakest retrieval slice at 0.38 even though the
  question names the book and chapter. That is the D3 target, and multi-hop does
  not explain it.
- EM is 0.00 across the board. Answers are sentences and references are short
  noun phrases, so EM measures brevity here. F1 is 0.28 after citations are
  stripped and sampling is pinned to temperature 0. Earlier D1 baselines counted
  citation ids as tokens and were not temperature-pinned; those F1 numbers are not
  comparable.
- Faithfulness is 11/15 (0.73), and all four failures sit outside `simple`
  (1 in named_reference, 3 in multi_part). Unsupported claims appear when an
  answer stitches two facts together.
- `multi_part` is also the weakest generation slice on correctness: 2/5 correct,
  2 partial, 1 wrong and 2/5 faithful. That points at multi-fact grounding, not retrieval
  alone.
- Abstention is at ceiling (10/10) with no abstention in the judged sample, so
  there is no measured over-refusal to buy back in D6.
- Not comparable with the V1 mini-eval (Hit@5 0.70). The V2 gold set is larger
  and includes the named and multi-part slices V1 did not have.
