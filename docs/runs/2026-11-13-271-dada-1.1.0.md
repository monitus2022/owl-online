## Reading notes

- The validator and every deterministic check pass. The independent solver
  selects the stored key on 25/25 and accepts every golden short answer.
- Option-length bias drops from 80.0% to 36.0%, with the mean gap moving from
  1.28 to -0.36 words. The correct option is no longer the longest in most
  items; nine items still have it longest, three are ties and thirteen have a
  longer distractor.
- The short-answer items, rubric and judge pin are unchanged, but the measured
  scores moved. The 1.0.0 record has exact 0.5833, adjacent 0.9167, MAE 0.5000,
  4 calls and 1 retry; 1.1.0 has exact 0.7083, adjacent 0.9167, MAE 0.3750,
  3 calls and 0 retries. The #191 stability band was MAE 0.4167-0.5000 with
  exact 0.5833, so this run sits outside it. The judge is not bit-stable on the
  frozen set; the DADA calibration should be re-baselined before the next
  comparison.
- The independent candidate diagnostic moved to 20/25; it is not gated and is
  not a bank change.
- The bank is local and gitignored. It was imported into
  `data/exams/exams.db`, uploaded out of band, and verified live.
- No chat, lesson, memory, retrieval or persona code changes are part of this
  revision.
