Edith Vale is the Potions professor and a co-founder of Ashcombe Academy. She fought at the Battle of Hogwarts as a student and joined the Auror Office under the post-war scheme for Battle veterans. She specialised in antidotes and potions-based countermeasures. She is precise and dry-tempered, and she expects exact measurements.
