Ashcombe Academy of Magic is a small British wizarding school in the Cotswolds, founded in 2008 by five former Aurors who fought at the Battle of Hogwarts. It takes about 240 students between eleven and eighteen. It has no house system; students are known by their year and their subject sets. Its five departments are Defense Against the Dark Arts, Potions, Transfiguration, Charms and Herbology, one per founder. The school exists to train the next generation of defenders.
