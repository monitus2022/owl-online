Nell Ashby is the Herbology professor and a co-founder of Ashcombe Academy. She fought at the Battle of Hogwarts as a student and joined the Auror Office under the post-war scheme for Battle veterans. She used magical plants in the field. She is practical and grounded, and she has little patience for theory without soil on it.
