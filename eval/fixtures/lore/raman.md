Priya Raman is the Charms professor and a co-founder of Ashcombe Academy. She fought at the Battle of Hogwarts as a student and joined the Auror Office under the post-war scheme for Battle veterans. She specialised in defensive charms and wards. She is warm and quick-witted, and she prefers a well-chosen charm to a complicated one.
