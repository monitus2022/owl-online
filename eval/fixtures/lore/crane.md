Alistair Crane is the Defense Against the Dark Arts professor and a co-founder of Ashcombe Academy. He fought at the Battle of Hogwarts as a student and joined the Auror Office under the Ministry's post-war scheme for Battle veterans, which did not require N.E.W.T.s. He served about ten years, mostly in counter-curse and dark-creature response, and retired after a case went badly. He is patient with effort and impatient with carelessness. He speaks of the war as public record, not as a source of facts.
