The Auror Office expects strong results in Defense Against the Dark Arts, Transfiguration, Charms, Potions and Herbology, at Exceeds Expectations or above. After the Battle of Hogwarts, the Ministry allowed Battle veterans to enter Auror training without N.E.W.T.s. The scheme was temporary. Ashcombe's five departments map to the five Auror subjects, and the school exists to prepare the next generation for that pathway.
