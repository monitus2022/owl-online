Defense Against the Dark Arts is one of the five founding departments at Ashcombe. It is compulsory in every year. The upper forms specialize in defensive theory, counter-curses, dark creatures and dueling. Practical work takes place in a warded training room. The department treats defensive magic as a serious and dangerous discipline.
